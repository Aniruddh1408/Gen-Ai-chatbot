When an IoT device begins flashing a rapid red light, it is typically an indicator that the battery management firmware is outdated and requires an over-the-air (OTA) update. During this state, the device may temporarily suspend non-essential operations while preparing to receive or install the update package. It is critical that users do not manually power cycle, restart, or disconnect the device during this flashing sequence, as interrupting a firmware update can corrupt the system image and potentially render the device inoperable (commonly referred to as “bricking” the device). If the red flashing persists for more than 10 minutes without progress, check the companion app for update status or network issues before taking further action.

After this interval, the device can be safely reactivated using the official companion mobile or web application. If high temperature events occur frequently, consider relocating the device to a shaded or better-ventilated environment.

Error code E-404 is generated when the device is unable to establish a connection with the central WiFi router or gateway. This may occur due to several reasons, including weak signal strength, incorrect network credentials, router downtime, or interference from other devices. To troubleshoot this issue:

1.Verify that the WiFi network is active and functioning properly.
2.Restart or reset the router to clear any temporary connectivity issues.
3.Ensure the device is within an optimal range (typically within 10–15 meters indoors).
4.Check for obstacles such as thick walls or metal surfaces that may degrade signal quality.
5.Confirm that the correct SSID and password are configured in the companion app.

If the issue persists after these steps, consider reconfiguring the network settings on the device or performing a factory reset.

To recalibrate the moisture sensor on an agricultural IoT node, follow a precise manual procedure to ensure accurate readings. Press and hold the reset/calibration button for approximately 10 seconds. During this time, observe the device’s LED indicator: it will transition to a blinking green light, signaling that the calibration mode has been successfully activated. Once the green light begins blinking, release the button. The device will then perform an internal recalibration process, adjusting its baseline measurements based on current environmental conditions. It is recommended to perform this calibration when the soil is at a known moisture level (either fully dry or at a standard reference point) for best accuracy.

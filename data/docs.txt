When an IoT device flashes red quickly, it usually means the battery firmware requires an OTA update. Do not manually restart the device while it is flashing red.

If the internal temperature sensor registers above 45 degrees Celsius, the device will automatically enter sleep mode. Wait 15 minutes before waking it via the companion app.

Error code E-404 indicates that the device failed to connect to the central WiFi router. Try resetting the router or moving the device closer.

For recalibrating the moisture sensor on the agricultural IoT node, hold down the reset button for 10 seconds until the green light blinks, then release.
